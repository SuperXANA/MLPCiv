# Sweet Dreams: Future Civilopedia Evolution Notes

> These notes are excluded from the active `LEADER_SWEETDREAMS` biography. The base entry is Aurora Borealis' first-person observation record, written under the pen-name Solais a Tuath while she was alive in Canterlot. Later material must identify its own source rather than expanding Aurora's knowledge retroactively.

## Base Entry Boundary

The initial Civilopedia state ends with Aurora's observations of Sweet Dreams: her origin, early uncertainty, self-chosen motherhood, self-naming, dream-home worldview, desire for companionship, and the limits Aurora could observe through the Yggdrasil lenses. It contains no knowledge of Prime Equestria, the Protection Committee, the World Forum, or Sweet Dreams' later classification as a Great Darkness.

## Possible Evolution: Contact with Prime Equestria

**Status:** Future lore annotation  
**Source:** Later Prime Equestrian and cross-reality records  
**Possible unlock:** A persistent event recording verified contact between Kinderquestria and Prime Equestria

Sweet Dreams understood Prime Equestria's inhabitants as "big foals" who had not yet realized that they could come home. She approached them through the maternal relationship she already believed existed, rather than through an ordinary claim to territory. Prime Equestria's Protection Committee answered by defending waking autonomy, civilizational independence, and the right of a pony to understand Sweet Dreams' invitation and still refuse it.

This annotation must preserve Prime Equestria as the canon-like G4 branch. It belongs to later sources and remains distinct from Queen Haven's New Equestria.

## Possible Evolution: Great Darkness Classification

**Status:** Future lore annotation  
**Source:** World Forum archival finding  
**Possible unlock:** A persistent event recording the Forum's examination of Sweet Dreams or the evidence surrounding Forever Naptime

The World Forum classified Sweet Dreams along the axis of Sleep and Oblivion. The designation describes a civilization-scale danger arising from her infinitely extensible care. If every departure is only a foal wandering outside and every pony must eventually come home, independent waking history becomes a temporary misunderstanding awaiting correction.

The classification establishes no secret cruelty, conventional conquest motive, memory-erasure power, hive mind, or automatic loss of identity. A Splinter or representative may participate in Forum proceedings; Sweet Dreams herself remains constrained within the Dreamscape unless later lore establishes another waking manifestation.

## Deferred Civilization Material

Kinderquestrian government, mayors and councillors, its voluntary economy, Good Foal Stickers, research practices, and exclusively nonlethal defense belong primarily in a future civilization-specific entry. They are consequences of choices made by individually minded Kinderquestrian ponies, rather than direct expressions of Sweet Dreams' personal will.

## Proposed Civilization Mechanic: Dreamscape Research Storage

**Status:** Unimplemented design proposal  
**Scope:** Kinderquestria as a civilization, separate from Sweet Dreams as an individual

A unique Building or repeatable Process could accumulate research beakers in a civilization-wide pool between Kinderquestrian turns. At the beginning of the next Kinderquestrian turn, the pool would be applied to the active technology and then reset to zero or retain a very small random remainder.

The mechanic represents distributed cooperation through the Dreamscape. It establishes no claim that research occurs only during sleep, that Sweet Dreams owns Kinderquestrian discoveries, that individual minds merge, or that Good Foal Stickers possess scientific value. Building versus Process, accumulation timing, reset behavior, and persistence rules remain unresolved until implemented.

## Identity and Scope Safeguards

- "Solais a Tuath" in the base entry is Aurora Borealis' authorial pen-name.
- The later summonable Solais a Tuath is an independently manifested egregore shaped by civilization's idea of that author. She is neither Aurora Borealis nor Sitelae.
- Sitelae is Aurora's later ghostly identity and does not narrate later events merely because they occur after Aurora's death.
- Prime Equestria and World Forum additions require explicit later sources.
- `CIVILIZATION_DREAM_EQUESTRIA` also contains Tantabus, Lullaby Love, Aurora Borealis, Emaduse Unistus, and Sitelae Astralues Aether. Evolved Sweet Dreams material must remain attached only to `LEADER_SWEETDREAMS`.
