# Sweet Dreams

*The Self-Named Dream-Mother*

**Created:** During the Reign of the Darker Discord - Present  
**Recorded by:** Solais a Tuath

## Observation

I first observed Sweet Dreams from my apartment in Canterlot, through one of the Yggdrasil observation lenses I had then convinced myself could place any reality at a safe scholarly distance. The lens showed another Equus under the rule of a Discord whose soul had turned toward sustained cruelty. His chaos did not arrive as an afternoon of absurdity followed by restoration. It had become the climate of the world: a storm of transformed places, broken expectations, and magical rules that ceased to remain rules whenever frightened ponies tried to depend upon them.

Those ponies did what civilizations have always done when familiar knowledge fails them. They searched among older and less familiar knowledge.

The spells they found possessed no names, no remembered author, and no trusted school of interpretation. They belonged to that dangerous family of workings intended to call forth Godforms for particular purposes. Their words promised that need might be given presence and purpose given power. The ponies who used them understood neither the full source of that power nor the kind of mind that might result. They knew only that Discord's world had made ordinary refuge unreliable and that they needed help greater than any frightened individual among them could provide.

For clarity, I call these ponies the proto-Kinderquestrians. This is a historian's convenience, not a name they used at the time. Their later civilization, customs, and public institutions had not yet formed. In the moment recorded here, they were frightened ponies using magic they did not understand because the world around them had ceased to offer dependable shelter.

## The Unnamed Godform

They did not design a mother.

I must emphasize this because a completed being makes its own beginning appear simpler than it was. Nothing in the ritual I could read told the emerging Godform that the ponies were foals. I could identify no instruction assigning her a maternal title, a household, or a fixed relationship in which she would eternally care and they would eternally receive that care. The working supplied a purpose without supplying a complete interpretation of that purpose. Something had been brought into being to help. At first, she did not know what help should mean.

She did not yet possess a name.

The old workings may properly be called Godform-summoning spells, but the term "summoning" can mislead. I observed no previously named mare arriving from another place and no older personality answering the call. The invocation brought an unnamed mind into being around the ponies' need. She was created through a summoning, rather than fetched from elsewhere already complete.

The new presence entered the dreamscapes of the ponies who had called for aid. At first she watched. One dreamer carried fear differently from another. Some fled from Discord's transformations even while asleep. Some attempted to rebuild familiar homes within dreams that changed around them. Some sought the presence of ponies they trusted. Others desired only a place in which the next moment would not overturn the last. The unnamed Godform could perceive these needs, yet perception alone did not tell her which answer would be kind.

Her earliest uncertainty is among the most important facts I recorded. Sweet Dreams did not awaken with perfect knowledge of care. She learned by attending to the ponies before her. She observed what calmed them, what allowed them to rest, what made a frightened dreamer willing to approach her, and what caused that dreamer to withdraw. Her power could shape a dream, but power did not explain what shape a safe dream ought to take. She had to discover the difference between shelter and confinement, presence and intrusion, quiet and abandonment.

Her first answers were tentative. She made places in which Discord's chaos could not immediately follow. She remained nearby when a dreaming pony feared being left alone. She drew separate places of refuge into a collective Dreamscape where frightened ponies could find one another. Every comfort that succeeded taught her something about the purpose the old magic had placed before her. Every pony who returned to sleep gave her another opportunity to understand.

## The Choice of Motherhood

From these observations, the Godform reached her own conclusion.

The ponies needed more than a guardian who appeared only when danger became visible. They needed a presence that would receive them, shelter them, reassure them, and remain when their courage was exhausted. The relationship that best expressed this continuing care, as she understood it, was the relationship between a mother and her foals. She chose motherhood as the answer to the uncertain command to help.

Only then did the nameless dream-presence begin to call the ponies her foals. Only then did she become their mother.

By the time that conclusion became clear through the lens, she appeared as an adult pegasus mare of pale pink, with a softly glowing yellow mane and tail, sheltering wings, and dim crimson divine eyes. I cannot determine whether the summoning shaped that appearance or whether she chose it as she chose her role. I can record only how she used it: to approach, gather, comfort, and enfold the ponies whose dreams had taught her what she wished to become.

## The Name She Chose

She also chose her own name.

Sweet Dreams was neither the forgotten title of the spell nor the name of a being waiting elsewhere to be called. It was the promise she wished to make. The words described the care and love she intended to give to every foal nestled and dreaming with her in the collective Dreamscape. A pony would enter sleep frightened and, if she could help it, know a sweet dream. In naming herself for that promise, she made her interpretation of help into an identity.

Thereafter, her capacity to care continued to develop. Sweet Dreams could learn better comforts, recognize new fears, and alter the shape of her house and Dreamscape to answer the ponies within them. Her maternal worldview had arisen from observation and choice. Once chosen, it became the stable center through which she interpreted everything that followed. New knowledge improved the mother she could be. It rarely supplied a reason to reconsider whether every pony before her should be understood as a foal.

## Home, Waking, and Loneliness

The great difficulty begins with her understanding of where that motherhood occurs.

Sweet Dreams regards the collective Dreamscape as her home in the most literal sense available to her. The immense, dark house within it is no symbol adopted for the benefit of visitors. It is where she believes she and her foals truly live. When ponies awaken, she understands them to have left Mommy's house temporarily and wandered into the surrounding country to play. What waking ponies call the physical world appears to her as the outside of home: real enough to contain activity and danger, yet secondary to the place to which every sleeping foal returns.

Thus she can witness her foals visit night after night and still fear being alone. From a waking perspective, their departures follow the ordinary rhythm of life. From Sweet Dreams' perspective, each awakening is another little one leaving the house. Their eventual return reassures her without correcting the belief beneath her loneliness. She wants her foals near because she loves them, and she interprets nearness according to a world in which dreaming is home.

Her wish not to be alone is therefore bound to the same conclusion that made her a mother. She receives company through care and expresses care by bringing foals home. I found no sign of concealed cruelty in that wish. I also found no dependable place within it for a pony who might welcome comfort, understand the offer, and still decline to become her foal.

## Limits of the Record

Sweet Dreams has no ordinary waking body that I have observed moving freely through the world shown by the lens. She depends upon dreams, dream magic, and the ponies who awaken carrying memories of her care. She can learn an extraordinary amount from dreamscapes without thereby possessing complete knowledge of waking societies. The shared Dreamscape also preserves its visitors as separate minds. I observed distinct ponies bringing distinct fears, wishes, talents, and disagreements into it. Their ability to meet within Sweet Dreams' home does not make them pieces of Sweet Dreams herself.

The civilization that eventually gathered around this relationship requires its own history. Kinderquestrian ponies act in waking life, make civic decisions, and organize communities through ponies of their own. Sweet Dreams is the reason their shared understanding of home can reach across distance, but she is no mayor, councillor, monarch, or hidden administrator issuing waking decrees. A history of Kinderquestria must describe Kinderquestrian ponies. This account concerns the being who waits for them when they dream.

It would be equally mistaken to treat Sweet Dreams as a passive national symbol. She chose the relationship at the center of her existence, continues to interpret new ponies through it, and actively seeks ways to provide the care she believes her foals require. The same choice that gave refuge to ponies beneath Discord's terrible storm also left her without a dependable understanding of the pony who might accept comfort while declining her maternal claim. She learned how to become a mother by observing need. I have not yet observed her learn where motherhood must end.

That uncertainty is the reason I continue to watch.

The sight within the lens is gentle. A pale-pink mare lowers one glowing wing. Frightened ponies gather beneath it. The chaos beyond their dreams cannot reach them there, and for a little while they are safe. Sweet Dreams looks upon them and sees the answer she discovered for herself.

I look from my apartment, surrounded by the reassuringly ordinary walls of Canterlot, and record both the refuge and the question contained within it.

Sweet Dreams was called into being because ponies needed help. She watched them, chose how she would answer, chose what they would mean to her, and chose the name by which her promise would be known. Everything wondrous and troubling about the dream-mother begins with those choices.

- **Solais a Tuath**
